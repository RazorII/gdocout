gdocout

Copy a Google Drive folder into files you can open anywhere, offline: Google Docs, Sheets and Slides become Word, Excel and PowerPoint (or PDF), everything else is copied as it is. gdocout never asks for a Google password: files shared “anyone with the link” come straight from Google, and the rest through your own web browser, signed in to Google.
